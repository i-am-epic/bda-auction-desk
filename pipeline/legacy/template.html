    How each site is rated

    Fair value starts from a 2026 realisable rate for a regular BDA-khata plot in that locality (asking prices × about 0.85). It is discounted for small, narrow or irregular plots and adjusted 3–4% for facing. If nobody bid on this exact site at an earlier base, fair value is capped at that base + 10%, grown at the area rate since. The market already said no at that price.

    Competition comes from locality demand, BDA's sell-through there, ticket size, plot usability, and whether this site went unsold or came back after closing. The expected winning bid rises from just over the minimum bid towards 97% of fair value as competition rises. Win chance is the odds that bidding our max beats every expected rival.

    Rating = 30% headroom + 15% area growth + 15% location + 15% usability + 15% data and surroundings confidence + 10% win chance. Any serious red flag caps it at 5.

    Before you bid

    • On KPPP, log in, open Auctions, search the auction number shown in each site's report, and express interest before 31 Oct, 5 pm. Pay the ₹4 L EMD and ₹500 fee per site through the portal.
    • Visit the plot. Check road width (it sets FAR and floors), slope, drains, encroachment and overhead lines. Sites are sold as is.
    • Your bid must be strictly more than base + 10%, and each site needs at least 2 bidders. If you are the only bidder, BDA rejects the sale.
    • Line up funds. You pay 25% (less EMD) within 72 hours of the advice letter, then the 75% balance within 45 days.
    • If the price is over ₹50 L, deduct and remit 1% TDS. Budget about 6.6% on top for stamp duty and registration.
    • For Arkavathy sites, ask BDA for the survey number and check it for re-do or denotification litigation.
